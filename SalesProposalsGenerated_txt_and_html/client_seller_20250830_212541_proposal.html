* Conduct stakeholder interviews to gather detailed insights into the current challenges and opportunities.

* Develop a detailed project plan with specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals.

* Create a work breakdown structure (WBS) outlining all tasks and sub-tasks.

* Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) to track progress and measure success.

* Identify and document all relevant stakeholders and their roles.

* Define communication channels and reporting frequency.

Phase 2: Requirements Analysis (Week 3-4)

* Conduct in-depth stakeholder workshops to gather detailed functional and non-functional requirements.

* Analyze existing business processes and workflows to identify areas for improvement.

* Translate business requirements into user stories and acceptance criteria.

* Develop detailed process maps and workflow diagrams.

* Document all requirements in a clear and concise format.

* Identify and mitigate potential risks and dependencies.

* Validate requirements with stakeholders to ensure alignment.

* Prioritize requirements based on business value and feasibility.

Phase 3: Technical Development (Week 5-8)

* Design and develop the technical architecture for the proposed solution.

* Develop the frontend and backend components of the solution.

* Implement database design and integration.

* Develop APIs for integration with existing systems.

* Conduct code reviews to ensure quality and maintainability.

* Implement security measures to protect sensitive data.

* Perform unit testing to ensure functionality.

* Adhere to coding standards and best practices.

Phase 4: Testing & Quality Assurance (Week 9-10)

* Develop a comprehensive test plan.

* Conduct functional, performance, and security testing.

* Document all test results and defects.

* Work with the development team to resolve defects.

* Perform user acceptance testing (UAT) with key stakeholders.

* Obtain sign-off from stakeholders on the final solution.

* Conduct regression testing to ensure stability.

* Ensure the solution meets all requirements and acceptance criteria.

Phase 5: Deployment & Implementation (Week 11-12)

* Develop a detailed deployment plan.

* Prepare the production environment for deployment.

* Deploy the solution to the production environment.

* Monitor the solution to ensure stability and performance.

* Provide user training and documentation.

* Provide ongoing support and maintenance.

* Gather user feedback and make necessary adjustments.

* Ensure a smooth and successful transition to the new solution.
